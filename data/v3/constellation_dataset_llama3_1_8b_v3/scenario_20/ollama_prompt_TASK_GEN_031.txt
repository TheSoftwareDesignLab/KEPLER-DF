Tasking Request for Routine Monitoring: Colombia's Yarumal Region

We require a standard operational tasking for routine monitoring of the Yarumal region in Colombia. This involves a multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared reflection to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index, as well as surface reflectance. The task is classified as a commercial tasking and part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status.

The mission allocation priority for this tasking is medium importance. We aim to capture the data sometime today around mid-day, specifically during the overnight period. Please ensure that the satellite resources are allocated accordingly to meet this requirement.
